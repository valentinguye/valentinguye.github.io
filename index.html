Valentin Guye

I do applied research on the economics of land resources. I am especially interested in the sustainability of tropical agriculture, related supply chains, and due diligence regulation.

I am a research fellow of France’s Institute for Research in Agriculture, food and Environment (INRAE), working at the Center for Environmental Economics of Montpellier (CEE-M).

Before, I worked at MCC Berlin, Paris Saclay Applied Economics, the Land Systems for Sustainability Lab, and Trase. I obtained my PhD in 2022, in economics from Université Paris-Saclay, and in geography from Humboldt Universität zu Berlin.

Guye is pronounced like Gi in Github. My pronouns are he/him.
